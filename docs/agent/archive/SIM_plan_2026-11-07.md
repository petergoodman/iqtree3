## Appendix A. The simulation scope Peter approved (2026-11-07), with reasons

**Purposes.** Simulation here serves three ends: checking that the simulator does what the model
says; measuring whether the estimator recovers a known Q and how much data that takes; and
measuring whether a misspecified fit invents non-reversibility. Held-out empirical evaluation
remains the primary evidence that a model is better (Trost et al. 2024: simulated protein data are
easy to tell from real ones, and composition alone separates them).

**S5: no simulation.** S5 compares two solvers on the same data; their agreement does not depend
on how the data were generated.

**SIM-1 (laptop).**
1. Simulator check. About a million sites on a two-taxon rooted tree, tip-pair counts against the
   oracle's exact probabilities (400 cells), with and without `+G4`. For a non-reversible Q the
   joint law depends on both branch lengths separately, so the test also checks the root
   distribution and root handling. Stronger and cheaper than a second simulator (Mendes et al.
   2024 ask for an independent check of the simulator). If AliSim fails, pass `+F{π*}` and retest.
2. Infinite-data recovery. The oracle's NQC objective fitted to exact expected pattern frequencies
   on 3- and 4-taxon trees must return the true Q. Separates estimator correctness from data needs.
3. Exact misspecification screen. The same expected-frequency fits show how much circulation each
   misspecification produces, with no simulation noise: a global target shift; per-gene
   compositions fitted with one π*; a profile-mixture truth; content-dependent residue masking.
   The design documents did this at three states (P-positive section 8, check 11). Rough cost: one
   to two hours per 360-parameter fit on 160,000 patterns in numpy (unverified).
4. Pipeline and pilot, setting replicate counts from Monte Carlo SEs (Morris et al. 2019). Output:
   SIM-2's plan, approved by Peter before any large run.

**SIM-2 (cluster).**
- Lean recovery: a truth family that dials circulation from zero to NQ.mammal strength at fixed
  composition (scaling the antisymmetric part of the balanced flux, within positivity; a proposal);
  30, 100, 300 and 1000 genes; mainly Level 1 on true trees, one size at Level 2; starts at LG
  rebuilt at π*, the GTR20 fit and a distant start (GTRpmix's lesson); per-rate error, net-flux
  error, held-out likelihood against the truth, ending rules under decision 039. Answers how many
  genes 360 parameters need. Pearson correlation alone hides large errors in small rates (Tinh et
  al. 2024: about 62 rates off by twofold at 100 genes despite r near 0.995; CherryML the same).
- Lean false circulation: finite-sample runs only for the scenarios the screen flags, plus the
  reversible truth at the correct target.
- Content-dependent masking against a known truth: residues removed as CLOAK removes them, then
  NQC at π*, frequency replacement and the reversible refit at π* compared with the truth. The
  only place the paper's central claim (frequency replacement loses stationarity and so estimates
  worse) gets known-truth evidence.

**S6.** Unchanged in content; may overlap SIM-2's cluster runs; uses SIM results (for instance,
whether the Level 3 driver is worth building).

**S7.** The confirmatory plan uses SIM-2's outputs. Two parametric bootstraps from the real data,
both already required by the design documents (P-positive section 8): simulate from the real
cleaned data's reversible fit and fit NQC at π* (the decisive null for any circulation claim);
simulate from the real NQC fit and check recovery. Resample genes to recompute π* and refit, as a
sensitivity analysis (D07 holds π* fixed for the main fit).

**Why the simulation slices come before S6.** They need none of S6's features; problems in how NQC
fits surface before more code is built on it; cluster compute can run while S6 is coded; results
inform S6 and S7's confirmatory plan (threshold, stopping setting, how much data the fit needs).

**Dropped, with reasons.**
- A 5% likelihood-ratio test of reversibility: at phylogenomic scale any misspecification makes it
  significant, and a 5% tail needs hundreds of full-scale replicates. Decision 041 instead.
- An arm with an independently estimated target: with tens of millions of residues the sampling
  error in π* is negligible; S7's gene resampling covers it.
- A second independent simulator: replaced by the exact pattern check.
- Full-workflow (Level 3) recovery: Tinh et al. 2024 did it for the unconstrained model, and the
  change here is confined to the matrix update.
- Full cleaning realism (simulated indels, Muscle5 ensembles, CLOAK): expensive, simulated indels
  are of doubtful realism, and it tests the cleaning paper's claim rather than NQC's.
- Non-stationarity along lineages: the model assumes stationarity; stated as a limitation.
- Simulating root placement accuracy: not needed for this project's claims, and untested for
  amino acids (Bettisworth and Stamatakis 2021), so a possible later paper. The root policy for
  scientific runs is separate and stays with G5 (decision 016).

**What Peter supplies** (risks 25 and 26 hold the open list). Most inputs are public and the agent
can fetch them once Peter chooses: NQ.mammal, Q.mammal, LG and the C20 and C60 profiles ship with
IQ-TREE, and AliSim is built in. Recommended defaults: the mammal data used by QMaker and nQMaker
(public on Dryad) for trees and per-gene settings, not OrthoMaM, which is the training and test
source; total net flux relative to total flux as the circulation statistic; CLOAK's removal
statistics from BAliBASE, where CLOAK was benchmarked.

## Appendix C. Cost extrapolation (rough, unverified)

From S3c's recorded figures (`CHANGELOG.md`, S3c entry): one evaluation costs 2.52 to 2.57 ms per
500-column alignment at 20 taxa and `-T 1`, linear in alignments; an NQC `-S` fit at a million
columns took about 0.8 hours on 32 cores at efficiency 0.65, which implies about 12,000
evaluations a fit. Assuming cost linear in taxa and columns:

- one NQC `-S` fit at 190 taxa, 1000 genes of 500 columns: 12,000 × 1000 × 2.55 ms × 9.5, about
  80 core-hours; a `-p` fit at 99 rounds roughly seven times that; memory roughly 50 GB (S3c's
  10.6 GB at a million columns and 20 taxa, scaled);
- SIM-2 at 50 taxa and 400-column genes (30 to 1000 genes, 20 replicates, two truths, two or three
  arms; false-circulation scenarios at about 50 replicates and 300 genes; masking similar): about
  5,000 to 10,000 core-hours, weeks on the laptop, so a cluster.

Nuisance updates, thread losses and the export diagnostic's evaluations are excluded; SIM-1's
pilot replaces these figures with measured ones.
