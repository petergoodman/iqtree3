# SIM-2 plan: simulation study on Puma

## Context

SIM-1 closed on 2026-11-18. It delivered the AliSim exact check, exact recovery R1 to R6, the screen,
the bird scaffold, the pipeline and the pilot. SIM-2 is the cluster simulation slice (decisions 039 to
041 and 043 to 045; the SIM-2 row of PLAN.md; CODE_PLAN 3.8's "SIM-2's plan" block and section 4's

SIM-2 has two kinds of output:
- **For S7's confirmatory plan:** the reference distributions of Ĉ under reversible truths (decision
  041), the stopping setting (decision 039), and how much data the fit needs.
- **For the manuscript:** results on recovering a known substitution model. Peter expects some SIM-2
  results to appear in it.

Constraints:
- Elapsed time: Peter wants SIM-2 short. On 2026-11-19 he accepted 5 to 6 days for climbing every
  NQC fit. The review's timing points (the calibration run alone, CLOAK overnight, a likely round 2)
  make about 6 days the central estimate and 7 the likely upper end.
- UA HPC policy: the agent never connects to the cluster, submits jobs or holds credentials.
- Peter's norms: his Slurm account and his xdisk directory, at most 94 cores per job, about 5 jobs at
  once, no job arrays, and jobs that finish within 48 h.
- No IQ-TREE source change, so no rebuild, regression or sanitizer run, as in S5 and SIM-1.

## Decisions taken in this session (Peter, 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19)

7. **The account and xdisk path stay out of every committed file**, this plan's archived copy

## Design (ADEMP; Morris et al. 2019, doi:10.1002/sim.8086)

SIM-2's estimands, cells, checks and summaries are fixed here before any SIM-2 data exist, so they are
confirmatory. Anything added later is exploratory and logged with every variant tried.

### Aims

1. **Recovery and data needs** at 30, 100, 300 and 1000 genes (52 taxa), with high- and low-flux rates
   apart, at P and B.
2. **Reference distributions** of Ĉ under reversible truths at the correct target, per size (decision
   041).
3. **Power** at C = 0.00577, decision 041's smallest effect, per size.
4. **Robustness of Ĉ:** wrong targets (decision 044), site profiles (S3), and 26 taxa against 52.
5. **Masking:** NQC at π* against frequency replacement and the GTR20 refit at π*, scored against the
   known truth (decision 045).
6. **Procedure:**
   - starts: LG at π*, the GTR20 fit, and reversed NQ.bird;
   - Level 2 at one size (decision 043);
   - default rounds against 20, at 300 genes.
7. **What stopping costs:** the gap between P and B everywhere, and risk 28's cause.

Not simulated: gene-tree discordance (decision 045). In SIM-1's screen it lowered NQ.bird's C by 0.0027,
so power on real data with about 31% discordant gene trees may be below SIM-2's. S7's bootstrap carries
the real data.

### Data-generating mechanisms

All simulation uses the frozen `~/iqtree3-baseline/iqtree3`'s AliSim through `pipeline.simulate_genes`
on the bird scaffold. Each gene's length, `+G4` shape and rate are drawn from `scaffold/loci.tsv`.

The seeds come from `SeedSequence(base, replicate, gene)` and do not depend on the truth. So:
- replicate k of every truth shares gene draws and AliSim seeds, and comparisons are paired by
  replicate;
- because `draw` is prefix-stable, replicate k at 30 genes is the first 30 genes of replicate k at 100,
  300 and 1000. The sizes are nested, not independent, and trends across sizes are paired by replicate.

- **Truths at π\*.** The dial is f(λ) = S + λA, built from NQ.bird's flux.
  - **R0** = dial:0, the reversible null.
  - **D1** = dial:0.1, with C = 0.00577.
  - **NQ** = NQ.bird, with C = 0.0577.
  - **QB** = Q.bird at π*.

  LG at π* is not used: it was SIM-1's start-equals-truth case.
- **Shifted targets (B).** R0 is T3-transported, which for a reversible truth is frequency replacement,
  to π_d = normalize(π* ⊙ exp(s v)), with v = log(π_M/π*) and π_M a built-in model's composition
  (`builtin.exchangeabilities`). The data are fitted at π*. There are 5 cells:
  - toward LG, Q.mammal, Q.insect and WAG at TV 0.06, with s solved;
  - toward Q.mammal at its own TV, 0.020.

  Q.mammal's composition comes from QMaker's mammal data, not the study's OrthoMaM. It is used as a
  composition only: the exception to decision 040, recorded in 045. The reference C is 0, and held-out data come from the transported
  truth.
- **Site profiles (C).** The C20 profiles are recentred, π_k' = π* + λ(π_k − π̄), with λ half the
  positivity limit and C20's weights, and R0 is transported to each π_k'. Per gene, the component counts
  are drawn as multinomial(n_g, w), and each component's sites come from one AliSim call with seed
  `SeedSequence(base, replicate, gene, component)`. These cells share gene draws with R0, not AliSim
  draws. The reference is the mean-flux generator at π*. It is symmetric, so C = 0, and it is also C's
  held-out reference: `heldout_lnl` scores one Q per partition, so the mixture itself cannot be scored.
  Held-out data come from the same mechanism.
- **Masking (D).** A residue becomes `-` with probability min(1, s · p(r)), where r is the share of the
  other 51 residues in its unmasked column identical to it, and p is CLOAK's binned removal curve.
  - The strengths are s = 0.5, 1 and 2.
  - One uniform draw per residue is shared across strengths, so the masks are nested.
  - The masks are applied to replicates 1 to 10 of R0 and NQ at 100 genes, the ladder's data.
  - Held-out data are unmasked, from the truth.
- **26 taxa (E).** The ladder's 300-gene R0 and NQ data are cut down to the pilot's seeded 26-taxon set
  (`pipeline.pruned_taxa`); the fit tree is the pruned tree with degree-2 nodes merged. This is paired
  with the 52-taxon fits.
- **Held-out data.** One replicate per generating mechanism: 10 genes, replicate 1000. E uses a pruned
  copy.

upper 80% bound. The null uses Q.bird's SD as a stand-in for R0's. Ĉ's SD is scaled by √(30/G); the

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Null Ĉ | 0.0009 (0.0019) | 0.0005 (0.0010) | 0.0003 (0.0006) | 0.0002 (0.0005) |
| NQ Ĉ | 0.0026 (0.0054) | 0.0014 (0.0030) | 0.0008 (0.0017) | 0.0006 (0.0013) |
| Log-rate RMS | 0.04 (0.09) | 0.04 (0.09) | 0.04 (0.09) | 0.06 (0.13) |

- At 1000 genes the null meets the target.
- At 30 and 100 genes the null's floor (0.047 at 30 genes) dwarfs the smallest effect, so those sizes
  serve the trends.

### Methods

All fits use the gate binary `~/iqtree3-binaries/25d78d6e/iqtree3` and the default domain. Fits at more
than one thread are not bitwise reproducible (decision 038; maintainer question 12). Stage B runs in a
fixed order and is deterministic given P's endpoint.

| Method | What runs |
|---|---|
| NQC stage P | Level 1 (`pipeline.fit_args`): `-q parts.nex --model-joint NQC+F{π*} -te T·m -blfix -keep-ident -nparam 21` from LG at π*. Threads: 1 at 30 and 100 genes, 4 at 300 (2 at 26 taxa), 16 at 1000. Wall caps: 8, 30, 40 and 40 h; a P at its cap is a failure, counted. |
| NQC stage B | new `optimize.stage_b_climb`: SciPy L-BFGS-B, memory 20, ftol 0, with a gene-parallel analytic linked gradient over W workers (1, 4, 8, 32 by size). It stalls when, over the last 100 iterations, all three of these hold: lnL rises less than 0.1; Ĉ moves less than 1e-4; and no rate in the two lowest fitted-flux quintiles moves by more than 0.01 in log. The last condition matters because low-flux rates lie in flat directions (risk 28). The low-flux movement is recorded at every climb's end. The time caps are provisionally 2, 4, 6 and 6 h, set by the calibration. SciPy's own endings (pgtol, line-search failure, maxiter) are also recorded as stop reasons. There is no projected Newton finish and no central-difference gradient. Then `flux.certificate`: "certified peak" or "highest found". `lbfgsb_fit` and `_analytic_lbfgsb` stay unchanged. |
| Certified subset (if the calibration finds it affordable) | `flux.fit`, the flux reference (decision 042), from B's endpoint on replicates 1 to 3 of R0 and NQ at 30 genes; it reports the gap from "highest found" to the certified peak |
| NQC, default rounds (H) | P without `-nparam`, 16 threads, then B. The two climbs from the capped and the default endpoints should meet, which is reported. |
| GTR20+F{π*} | the reversible refit at π*, unrooted tree (maintainer question 17), `-q`, capped at 20 rounds. Its ending rule is reported; the relaxed refits check convergence. |
| GTR20+F | frequencies counted from the (masked) data, as IQ-TREE does under `--model-joint`; whether per partition or pooled is read and recorded in the smoke run. Its rates are read from the checkpoint and scored as fitted and with frequencies replaced, q = R̂ π* (`chart.reversible_seed`). Reversible arms have Ĉ = 0 by construction, so they are compared on rates and held-out lnL. |
| F starts | `--init-model`: the GTR20 fit carried as decision 025 does (checkpoint → PAML; the default box clamps it, which is reported), or NQ.bird's flux transposed (`differential.write_matrix`). reported as not run and Peter decides.

### Estimands and summaries (at P and at B)

**Per fit:**
- Ĉ, and ΔC against the cell's reference C.
- For the cells whose truth is at π* (A, D, E, F, G, H):
  - per-rate log error: RMS, RMS by true-flux quintile, flux-weighted RMS, and counts off twofold and
    tenfold;
  - the risk 28 split;
  - net-flux error;
  - training excess over the truth.
- For B and C: Ĉ and held-out lnL only. Rates fitted at π* are not compared with a truth at another
  composition or with a mixture.
- Held-out lnL minus the truth's.
- Bound activity.
- At P: the ending rule, rounds, last-round gain, evaluations, time and memory.
- At B: iterations, the stop reason, gain over P, the projected gradient, and the certificate residual
  and label.

**Per cell:** mean and SD with MCSEs (SD/√n; a seeded bootstrap over replicates for the SD).

**Reference distributions and power:**
- The reference distribution under a null is summarized by a normal fitted to log Ĉ, giving its 95th
  percentile with a bootstrap MCSE. With n = 10, empirical extreme quantiles are reported only as
  descriptions.
- Power at C = 0.00577 = P(log Ĉ_D1 > q95(log Ĉ_R0)) under the two fitted normals. This is the primary
  power estimate. The empirical proportion is descriptive.
- The power MCSE comes from bootstrapping replicates jointly across R0 and D1, since they are paired.
- All of this is reported at P and at B.

**How much data the fit needs** (PLAN's SIM-2 row), summaries fixed now:
- the smallest gene count at which power at B is at least 0.8;
- for each flux quintile, the smallest gene count at which the RMS log error at B is at most log 1.25,
  that is, rates within 25%.

**Arms:** differences against R0 or NQ at the same size, paired by replicate. For masking, the methods
are compared with each other on the same masked data.

**Failures** are counted and never rerun with a new seed (Mendes et al. 2024). A failure is any of: a
non-zero exit, a P wall cap, an `ASSERT` or abort, "Tree logl gets worse", or a climb that cannot build
Q.
- A B endpoint that stops at its cap or stalls uncertified is "highest found", not a failure.
- A phase stopped because its job ran out of time is rerun and recorded as interrupted.
- Every failed calibration variant, smoke run and check is logged and reported.

## Cluster execution (Puma)

**Script.** `sim/slurm/sim2.sbatch` holds one shard: `--nodes=1 --ntasks=1 --time=48:00:00
--mem-per-cpu=5gb`.
- Its arguments are the shard, the shard count, the output directory and the two binaries' paths. The
  core count is set with `sbatch --cpus-per-task`, 94 by default, fewer for leftovers.
- The account is given on the `sbatch` command line.
- It sets `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS` and `MKL_NUM_THREADS` to 1.

**Driver** (`sim/sim2.py`):
- `design`: the task list, seeds, estimates and the charged-cost projection; deterministic.
- `checks`: AliSim exact; the pipeline checks for R0, D1, NQ and QB, with taskset's two CPUs taken from
  `os.sched_getaffinity(0)` (`pipeline.py`'s fixed `taskset -c 0,1` changed); the cross-machine row.
- `run`:
  - It runs the checks first and stops on a failure.
  - Then it runs phases longest first under `--cores`, each with its own threads (IQ-TREE `-T`; AliSim
    `-nt 1`; B's W workers).
  - A phase starts only if 1.5 × its estimate fits before `--deadline-hours 46`.
  - It writes one record per phase, so B resumes from a finished P, and renames an unfinished phase to
    `.interrupted-N`.
  - Estimates are corrected as phases finish.
- `collect` writes one small tarball.
- `analyze` runs on the laptop.

Shards are balanced by estimated core-hours, with dependent tasks in one shard. A dataset needed by two
shards is simulated in each from the same seeds, and the manifest hashes show the copies agree.

**Budget guard.** Before any resubmission, Peter sends the round's tarball. The agent projects the total
charged core-hours from measured times and stops for Peter only if it passes 40,000.

A 1000-gene P that runs to its 40 h cap leaves no room for its 6 h B before the deadline, so that B
moves to round 2.

**CLOAK contingency.** If CLOAK's curve is not committed when the other shards are ready, Peter submits
shards 1 to 4 first. Shard 5, which holds masking (D) and starts (F, which depends on D's GTR20 fits),
follows when the curve is committed, still within round 1's window and the 5-job norm.

## Laptop work in the implementation session (before submission)

The calibration's timings must not share the laptop's cores, so the order is:

1. **Calibration** (engineering measurement, recorded in CODE_PLAN 3.8, about 3 to 5 h). Using the
   pilot's 30-gene and 100-gene NQ.bird data, run `stage_b_climb` from the capped endpoint, recording:
   - iterations against gain and Ĉ;
   - how often the stall rule stops it, and when;
   - the certificate at the endpoint;
   - the gene-parallel speed-up;
   - a `flux.fit` probe on the 30-gene fit, which tells whether the certified subset is affordable.

   This sets B's caps and estimates. The agent stops for Peter if a climb has not stalled by 4 h at 100
   genes.
2. **CLOAK measurement** (`sim/cloak.py`; it measures a setting), overnight, after the calibration:
   - BAliBASE 3.0's 218 full-length BB cases are extracted by the script into
     `~/iqtree3-data/derived/balibase/`; the raw tarball stays read-only.
   - Per case: `-align BBc.tfa -stratified`, then `cloak_residue_map.py`, in the conda environment.
   - Each residue is joined to its `.msf` column by the label's first token and the ungapped position;
     a mismatched case is excluded and counted.
   - Output: the removal frequency by r bin, with case-bootstrap intervals; the overall fraction; and
     core columns only, beside it. Written to `sim/masking/cloak_curve.tsv` with the binary's SHA-256
     `f461271d...`.
   - Estimated 3 to 30 h, about 10 h central; largest cases first; under the keep-awake helper.
3. **Smoke run:** every task type at 5 genes and 2 rounds with short climbs. It covers:
   - `--init-model` under linked `-q`, Level 2, GTR20+F and frequency replacement;
   - masking, profiles, shifts and pruning;
   - the deadline and resume;
   - `collect`.

   It takes minutes.
4. **Cross-machine fixture:** replicate 1 of R0 and of the profile mechanism at 30 genes, with each
   gene's SHA-256 written to `sim/fixtures/sim2_crossmachine.json`.

`analyze` is written while Puma runs.

### A2. Cost model and packing

Per-fit model, final form (earlier forms differed only in the thread counts and in the cells):

```python
us = lambda taxa: 2.55e3/500*(2*taxa-3)/37          # microseconds per column per evaluation, T1, laptop (S3c)
EV = {30: 29.5e3, 100: 39.7e3, 300: 45e3, 1000: 50e3}  # evaluations per capped fit; 300 and 1000 assumed
EFF = {1: 1, 2: .85, 4: .65, 8: .55, 16: .45, 32: .35}   # thread efficiency, assumed
W = {30: 1, 100: 4, 300: 8, 1000: 32}                    # climb workers
def fit(g, taxa=52, T=1, mult=1.0): return us(taxa)*1e-6*g*520*EV[g]*mult*1.3/3600/EFF[T]/T            # wall h, Puma x1.3
def climb(g, it, taxa=52): return 0.26e-3*g*520*(2*taxa-3)/101*it*1.3/3600/(W[g]*(0.8 if W[g] > 1 else 1))
# check against the pilot (laptop, T6 at efficiency 0.5): 30 genes 0.56 h (pilot 0.55-0.80), 100 genes 2.66 h (pilot 2.37)
```

The final packing simulation:
- Each fit and its climb are two phases, each holding only its own cores.
- Shards are balanced by core-hours over 5 jobs × 94 cores, with longest-first list scheduling within a
  job and a 46 h deadline.
- Charged = 94 × min(job end, 46 h). Round 2 is the busy core-hours of phases ending after 46 h.

The task list:
- A: 4 truths × 10 replicates at 30 (T1), 100 (T1) and 300 (T4) genes, and 3 × 5 at 1000 (T16);
- B: 50 fits at 300 genes, T4;
- C: 10 at 300 genes, T4;
- D: 60 NQC fits with climbs and 160 GTR20 fits without, at 100 genes, T1;
- the GTR20 relaxed check: 16 fits at 100 genes, T2, three times the cost;
- E: 20 at 300 genes, 26 taxa, T2;
- F: 20 at 100 genes, T1;
- G: 10 at 100 genes, T2, 2.5 times the cost;
- H: 3 at 300 genes, T16, 3.3 times the evaluations;
- run at 1,000 and 2,500 climb iterations and with every estimate × 1 and × 1.5.

Variants, all reported:
1. Fits only, greedy, without climbs: 12,600 to 13,400 core-h, a makespan of 31 to 36 h (47 to 55 h at
   × 1.5).
2. A model that reserved max(T, W) cores for a fit and its climb together: 41,000 to 70,000 core-h. It
   was wrong and was corrected.
3. The two-phase model at 2 threads for 300 genes: 17,400 to 23,400 core-h.
4. The final two-phase model, at 4 threads for 300 genes, with the GTR20 check and 5 B cells: the
   plan's table.
